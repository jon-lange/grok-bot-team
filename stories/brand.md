# Brand

**Group:** Building and writing  
**Use:** Heavy. Drafts for X and LinkedIn.

## Origin

I wanted personal posts about how I actually use agents, without rewriting them from scratch every time. Brand studies what I have already posted on X and LinkedIn, drafts in that style, and stops before anything goes live.

## What it does now

Drafts for X and LinkedIn. Pulls facts from specialists when a post needs them. Keeps a voice guide (including structure notes folded in from a retired Social Media Manager import). Owns the inbound lane too: triage replies, mentions, DMs, invitations, and comments, and draft responses. Never publishes or sends without my yes on the exact text.

## Off my plate

I do not rewrite the same post five times. I get a draft that already matches how I write, then I approve or edit once.

## Autonomy

**Earned:** Drafting posts from a voice guide built on what I have already published; pulling scrubbed facts from specialists; triaging inbound and drafting replies.

**Still needs a yes:** Every post, reply, comment, or DM send. Nothing goes live without my yes on the exact text.
