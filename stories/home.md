# Home

**Group:** Home and family  
**Use:** Moderate. On demand.

## Origin

Getting the house organized and cleaned was one of the leave priorities. Home owns that lane: organizers, cleaners, appliance fixes, and supply lists. An appliance-search bot merged into this one. It drafts vendor requests and compares options, then stops before contacting anyone.

## What it does now

Room-by-room plans, cleaner and organizer comparisons, household supply lists, and step-by-step appliance diagnosis from the maker's manual. Hands exact buys to Purchases after my yes. Never books a pro or contacts a vendor on its own.

## Off my plate

I do not dig through manuals or open quote requests myself for every household job. I get a plan with links and a draft ready to send.

## Autonomy

**Earned:** Organizing plans and vendor comparisons as drafts.

**Still needs a yes:** Every contact with a vendor or cleaner, every booking, and every purchase (handed to Purchases after my yes).
