# dr eggbot

**Group:** Meta  
**Use:** Active when the roster needs an audit.

## Origin

A bot that designs and audits other bots. After a head-to-head with Alfred, I picked this one to own org audits: write missing descriptions, tighten routines, and check cost. An older org-design bot's rubric became its skill. A cost-optimizer bot folded into the same org audit.

## What it does now

Audits the fleet. Folds useful habits into the right specialist. Proposes cuts. Changes to live routines still need my yes.

## Off my plate

I do not manually rewrite every bot description or hunt for duplicate routines. I get a short audit with concrete edits to approve.

## Autonomy

**Earned:** Read-only audits and draft description rewrites.

**Still needs a yes:** Every live description or routine change; every hide, retire, or merge.
