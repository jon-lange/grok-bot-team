# Purchases

**Group:** Home and family  
**Use:** Active. Buying plus a watch list.

## Origin

Shopping was another early specialist. It finds, compares, and buys personal goods and subscriptions I ask for. Draft only until I approve the exact item, price, and payment method.

A separate Watches bot used to scout one daily listing search. That habit folded into Purchases as a single alert-only routine. One bot, one shopping lane.

## What it does now

Product search and comparison. Ready-to-approve buy cards. Optional alert-only watch lists that stay quiet when nothing new matches. Hands completed buys to Expense Manager for logging. Never pays without my yes.

## Off my plate

I do not run the comparison tabs or re-check the same listing hunt every morning. Matches come to me once. Paying still needs my yes every time.

## Autonomy

**Earned:** Product search and comparison; alert-only watch lists that stay quiet when nothing matches; ready-to-approve buy cards.

**Still needs a yes:** Every payment; every bid, offer, or message to a seller.
