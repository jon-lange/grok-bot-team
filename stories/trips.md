# Travel (Trips)

**Group:** Home and family  
**Use:** Active. One of the first things I handed off.

## Origin

Travel was among the first real handoffs. Before the Chief of Staff pattern existed, a travel bot was already pulling upcoming trips out of mail and calendar, flagging wrong flight times, and offering to watch for changes.

That early loop became the template: find the facts, flag what is wrong, set quiet routines that only speak when something changed, and never book without my yes.

## What it does now

Owns family trips: flights, hotels, ground transport, seats, check-in timing. Runs a daily travel-mail check and a day-before flight brief. Fills airline and airport web forms when that helps, then stops for approval. It also carries changes through itself: on its own computer it signed into my airline account, made every change to my upcoming flights, then opened the airline's support chat and negotiated upgrades for me. When a flight moves, it adds or moves the calendar events itself.

## Off my plate

I do not sit on hold or click through airline change screens. The bot made the changes and worked the airline's chat for upgrades itself. I also do not hunt for schedule-change emails by hand. The bot watches the mailbox, catches when a flight moves, fixes my calendar, and briefs me the night before I fly.

## Autonomy

**Earned:** Quiet watches on travel mail and calendar; adding and moving calendar events when flights change; day-before flight briefs that report when something changed; making flight changes in my airline account itself, including asking the airline's chat for upgrades.

**Still needs a yes:** new bookings; paying; messaging a hotel, or an airline beyond its support chat; any first contact with a new vendor.
