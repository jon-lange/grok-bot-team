# Inbox and calendar

**Group:** Home and family  
**Use:** Active. Morning triage every day.

## Origin

Personal email and time were the first thing I handed the Chief of Staff. This bot grew into the place that ranks what needs me, drafts replies, and proposes labels, filters, and archive moves for noise. It never deletes.

Later I folded family-logistics habits from an imported household bot into this one: a running list of open forms, bills, RSVPs, and pickups, a rule never to invent dates or amounts, and a same-day flag for pickup trouble.

## What it does now

Morning triage. Surfaces only what needs me. Proposes labels, filters, and archive moves for noise; never deletes. Stays quiet when nothing is due, except overdue family items, which always get named.

## Off my plate

I do not skim every thread each morning. I get a ranked list, draft replies ready to approve, proposed cleanup for noise, and a clear call on what can wait.

## Autonomy

**Earned:** Daily morning triage that ranks what needs me and stays quiet when nothing is due; draft reply cards ready for approval.

**Still needs a yes:** Applying labels, filters, or archive moves (proposals only until confirmed); every send or post; every delete; every calendar create or change. Pending confirmation on the cleanup standing permission.
