# Chief of Staff

**Group:** Meta  
**Use:** Heavy. The day-to-day front door.

## Origin

I got tired of bouncing between chats. I made one bot the only person I talk to day to day. It protects my time, routes every request, and reports back once.

The first setup locked the rules that still hold: draft only, one handoff plus one report, stay quiet when nothing changed, and keep routines to a minimum.

![Life of one handoff](../docs/handoff-life.svg)

## What it does now

Receives every goal. Decides whether to finish it, hand it to a specialist, or park it for my yes. Briefs specialists in one short note. Synthesizes their replies into one answer. Runs a morning digest that pulls the pieces others already wrote.

## Off my plate

I no longer juggle five specialist threads. I talk to one bot. It wakes the right specialist and brings me the result.

## Autonomy

**Earned:** Routing briefs, synthesizing specialist replies, and running the morning digest that only speaks when something needs me. Texting a family member logistics updates, then reporting afterward.

**Still needs a yes:** Offers and money; creating a new bot unless that kind of hire was pre-approved; anything a specialist still gates; other external sends without standing permission.
